# voice/ — intentionally empty

NINETY-TWO TURNS is wordless by design (FINAL_SCREENPLAY: "no dialogue, no
narration and no on-screen text"; SHOT_LIST: "none - the film is wordless
by design"; CHARACTER_BIBLE on WICK: "Mouth: None. Deliberately and
permanently. All 'speech' in the film is sound design.").

No voice recordings exist, are planned, or may be added. Character voice
identity, emotional delivery and vocal timing are carried by designed
sound — see `../DIALOGUE_PLAN.md`. The dialogue track in
`../AUDIO_TIMELINE.json` is empty by design and the `wordless` check in
`../scripts/run_audio_tests.py` enforces it.
