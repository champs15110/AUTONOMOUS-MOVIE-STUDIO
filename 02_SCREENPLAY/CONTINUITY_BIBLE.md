## 3. CAUSE AND EFFECT ACROSS SCENES

Nothing in this film happens without a cause established earlier. The chain:

| # | Cause | Shot | Effect | Shot |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The ratchet slips and a tooth shears | `SC01_SH001–002` | She has 92 turns and no way to make more | `SC02` |
| 2 | The pillar socket is stripped | `SC02_SH002` | She must carry her own energy to the tower | `SC03` |
| 3 | Movement spends turns | `SC03_SH001–002` | The audience learns to read the gauge as a receipt | all |
| 4 | The street is flooded at her scale | `SC04` | She is damaged, and the shoulder joint starts to fail | `SC04_SH006` |

**No disconnected beats.** Every scene is entered from a consequence of the previous one, and every scene exits on a state change that the next scene must honour.

## 8. THINGS THAT MUST NEVER CHANGE

1. **92.** The number, and the arithmetic that spends it.
2. **One turn at the end.** Not a restoration. A beginning.
3. **The socket.** The climax is mechanical, not magical. No wick, no oil, no flint.
4. **Zero dialogue.** Any spoken line requires an explicit logged deviation.
5. **The pole's absence** between `SC05_SH003` and `SC07_SH005`.
6. **The loop.** `SC10_SH004` mirrors `SC01_SH001`.
7. **The single full-score moment** at `SC08_SH007`. Nowhere else.
8. **The child's face is never resolved.** Backlit, rim-lit, silhouette only.
