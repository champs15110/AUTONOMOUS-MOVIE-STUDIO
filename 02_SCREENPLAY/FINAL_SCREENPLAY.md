## LOGLINE

> A wind-up lamplighter whose charging pillar has broken has exactly ninety-two turns of stored energy left, and one night to light the harbour beacon before the last ship home is wrecked on the reef. Every step costs a turn. The beacon needs a mainspring, not a match.
