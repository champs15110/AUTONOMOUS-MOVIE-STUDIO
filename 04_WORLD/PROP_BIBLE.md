# PROP BIBLE — NINETY-TWO TURNS

**Project:** AMS-2026-001 · **Task:** T03_CHARACTER_WORLD · **Machine twin:** `WORLD_MANIFEST.json` · **Prop sheet:** `04_WORLD/REFERENCE_IMAGES/PROPS_master_sheet.png`

Every prop carries design, scale, material, purpose and continuity requirements. Continuity requirements are enforced by `02_SCREENPLAY/verify_shot_list.py` and must be re-checked at QC.

### the winding key

**Design.** T-shaped brass key, worn square drive, rounded bow.

**Scale.** ~12 cm - large relative to WICK, small in a human hand.

**Material.** worn brass

**Purpose.** Her charging interface; the film's opening and closing image; the object handed across the whole story.

**Continuity requirements.**
- Seated in her back SC01-SC10_SH002.
- Removed by the child SC10_SH003; in the child's hand SC10_SH003-005.
- The same asset mirrors SC01_SH001 and SC10_SH004.

### the chest gauge

**Design.** Round brass-cased clock gauge set beside the mainspring inside the chest drum; white enamelled dial, black needle, RED ARC painted on the lower portion.

**Scale.** ~6 cm dial, legible at shot scale.

**Material.** brass, enamel, steel needle

**Purpose.** The diegetic countdown - the film's spine.

**Continuity requirements.**
- Needle value driven by the shot list gauge fields - it is animated data, not a texture.
- Red arc entered at SC06_SH003; never before.
- Framing is locked: same lens/angle/key for every insert.

_Reference: 04_WORLD/REFERENCE_IMAGES/PROPS_master_sheet.png_

### the mainspring

**Design.** Blued-steel coiled clock mainspring, loosely wound, with one tooth visibly sheared off.

**Scale.** Fills the chest drum; matches the socket diameter exactly.

**Material.** blued steel

**Purpose.** Her energy source and, at the climax, the fuel she gives the beacon.

**Continuity requirements.**
- Sheared tooth from SC01_SH002 onward.
- Inside the chest SC01-SC08_SH005; installed in the burner from SC08_SH006; cannot be put back.

_Reference: 04_WORLD/REFERENCE_IMAGES/PROPS_master_sheet.png_
